Stardew Valley contains interconnected outdoor, indoor, dungeon, and special locations.

## Pelican Town
Pelican Town is the main town area and contains many shops, homes, services, and event locations.

## Farm
The farm is the player's primary property. Crops, animals, buildings, machines, trees, and other farm infrastructure can be placed there.

## Beach
The Beach is a major fishing and foraging location and contains Willy's Fish Shop.

## Mines
The Mines are a major mining and combat location in the Mountain area. Players can obtain ores, minerals, geodes, and monster drops there.

## Skull Cavern
Skull Cavern is a deeper dungeon area in the Calico Desert and is designed for mining and combat.

## Ginger Island
Ginger Island is a major late-game area with its own exploration, farming, characters, and progression systems.

## Volcano Dungeon
The Volcano Dungeon is a dungeon on Ginger Island with combat, mining, puzzles, and special progression.

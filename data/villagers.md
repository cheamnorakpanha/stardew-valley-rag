# Villagers

## Overview
Villagers are characters who live in and around Pelican Town. They have daily routines and can be affected by time, weather, events, and friendship.

## Friendship
Friendship is increased or decreased through interactions such as talking and giving gifts. Each villager has individual gift preferences.

## Daily routines
A villager's location can change throughout the day. Weather and special events can also change routines.

# Cooking and Recipes

## Overview
Cooking allows the player to turn ingredients into prepared dishes. Recipes can be learned through multiple sources, including television, friendship, shops, and other gameplay progression.

## Recipe record fields
For RAG, store:
- recipe name
- ingredients
- source of recipe
- cooking location
- sell price
- energy
- health
- buffs
- duration
- gift information

## Examples

### Pumpkin Soup
- Main ingredient: Pumpkin
- Main ingredient source: Pumpkin crop
- Type: Cooking recipe
- Useful for food effects and gifting depending on the villager.

### Pumpkin Pie
- Main ingredient: Pumpkin
- Type: Cooking recipe

### Autumn's Bounty
- Uses Fall-themed ingredients.
- Provides a food buff.

## Cooking system
The player needs access to a kitchen or another valid cooking setup. Ingredients can be stored in the refrigerator and used for cooking.

## RAG note
Recipes are best represented as structured chunks because ingredient-based questions are common:
"Which recipes use pumpkin?"
"What ingredients do I need for Pumpkin Soup?"

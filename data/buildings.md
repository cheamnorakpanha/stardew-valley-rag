# Buildings

## Overview
Buildings provide housing, production, storage, utility, and progression functions.

## Farm buildings
Important farm buildings include:
- Coop
- Barn
- Silo
- Stable
- Shed
- Mill
- Fish Pond
- Slime Hutch
- Junimo Hut
- Well
- Obelisks
- Shipping Bin
- Farmhouse

## Coop progression
Coops have upgraded forms that support more animals and provide additional functionality.

## Barn progression
Barns have upgraded forms that support more animals and unlock additional animal types.

## Farmhouse
The farmhouse can be upgraded. Upgrades expand the player's living space and unlock additional home functionality such as a kitchen.

## Greenhouse
The Greenhouse is an important farming building that enables year-round crop growing.

## Building construction
Robin's Carpenter Shop provides construction and upgrade services for many farm buildings.

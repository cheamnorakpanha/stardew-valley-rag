# Animals

## Overview
Farm animals provide products that can be sold, cooked with, gifted, or processed into artisan goods.

## Common farm animals
Examples include:
- Chicken
- Duck
- Rabbit
- Cow
- Goat
- Sheep
- Pig
- Ostrich

## Animal care
Animals require regular care. Important factors include:
- feeding
- housing
- friendship
- happiness
- access to appropriate buildings

## Animal buildings
Animals live in farm buildings such as:
- Coop
- Big Coop
- Deluxe Coop
- Barn
- Big Barn
- Deluxe Barn

## Products
Animal products can include:
- Eggs
- Large Eggs
- Duck Eggs
- Milk
- Large Milk
- Goat Milk
- Wool
- Rabbit's Foot
- Truffles
- Other special products

## Artisan processing
Examples:
- Milk -> Cheese
- Large Milk -> Large Cheese
- Eggs -> Mayonnaise
- Duck Egg -> Duck Mayonnaise
- Wool -> Cloth
- Truffles -> Truffle Oil

## 1.6 Meadowlands Farm
The official 1.6 update added the Meadowlands Farm type. It starts with a coop and two chickens and has special blue grass that animals love.

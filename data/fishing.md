# Fishing

## Overview
Fishing is one of the major activities in Stardew Valley. Fish can be caught in different bodies of water and under different conditions.

## Fishing locations
Important fishing environments include:
- River
- Lake
- Ocean
- Farm ponds
- Special fishing locations

## Fishing conditions
A fish's availability can depend on:
- location
- season
- time of day
- weather
- fishing zone
- special conditions

## Equipment
Fishing-related items include:
- Fishing rods
- Bait
- Tackle
- Crab Pots

## Fish ponds
Fish can be placed in Fish Ponds. Fish Ponds can produce fish-related items and may request additional items as their population grows.

## Fish data for RAG
For each fish, store:
- name
- location
- season
- weather
- time
- difficulty
- sell price
- uses
- bundle/quest use

## 1.6 fishing additions
The official 1.6 changelog added fish frenzies and introduced fishing festivals including Trout Derby and SquidFest.

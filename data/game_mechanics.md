# Game Mechanics

## Overview
Stardew Valley is an open-ended country-life RPG. The player can pursue farming, fishing, foraging, mining, combat, crafting, cooking, relationships, quests, museum collection, and Community Center/Joja progression.

## Day cycle
A day advances through a time-of-day system. The player has limited time each day and must eventually return to sleep.

## Energy
Many actions consume energy. Food and other mechanics can restore or modify energy.

## Health
Health is especially important during combat and dangerous activities.

## Skills
Major skills include:
- Farming
- Mining
- Foraging
- Fishing
- Combat

Skills gain experience from related actions and can unlock professions.

## Friendship
Talking, gifting, events, and other interactions can change friendship with villagers.

## Economy
Items can be sold for gold. Different processing machines can increase the value of raw farm products.

## Community Center
The Community Center provides bundles that require specified items. Completing bundles restores different parts of the Community Center and unlocks rewards/progression.

## Joja route
Players can instead purchase development projects through Joja, replacing the bundle-based Community Center route.

## Multiplayer
Stardew Valley supports multiplayer on supported platforms. Farmhands can share a farm and participate in many activities together.

## 1.6
The official 1.6 update added major features including:
- Mastery system
- Meadowlands Farm
- Desert Festival
- Trout Derby
- SquidFest
- new dialogue
- new items and crafting content
- numerous balance and quality-of-life changes

## Current version note
The official wiki's main page identifies version 1.6.15 for PC and several other current platforms, while platform-specific versions can differ.

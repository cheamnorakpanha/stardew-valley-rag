# Mining

## Overview
Mining provides ores, minerals, geodes, stone, and other materials. It is also connected to combat because many mining areas contain monsters.

## Major mining areas
- The Mines
- Skull Cavern
- Quarry Mine
- Volcano Dungeon

## The Mines
The Mines are divided into areas with different enemy and resource types. Progressing deeper changes the available resources and enemies.

## Skull Cavern
Skull Cavern is located in the Calico Desert and provides access to deeper and more valuable mining opportunities.

## Volcano Dungeon
The Volcano Dungeon is located on Ginger Island and includes combat, mining, puzzles, gates, and a forge-related end section.

## Mining resources
Common resource concepts include:
- Stone
- Copper Ore
- Iron Ore
- Gold Ore
- Iridium Ore
- Coal
- Geodes
- Minerals
- Gems

## Mining skill
Mining contributes to the Mining skill, which unlocks skill professions and improves mining-related efficiency.

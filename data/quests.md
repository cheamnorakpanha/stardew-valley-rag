# Quests

## Overview
Quests provide goals and rewards. They can come from villagers, the journal, special characters, or story progression.

## Quest-related systems
Important quest concepts include:
- Help Wanted quests
- Special Orders
- Story/progression quests
- Qi-related challenges
- Item delivery
- Gathering quests
- Combat quests
- Fishing quests

## Quest data for RAG
For each quest, useful fields include:
- quest name
- giver
- trigger
- season/date
- objective
- required items
- location
- deadline
- reward
- follow-up

## Examples of quest concepts
- Villagers can request items through Help Wanted quests.
- Special Orders provide larger objectives and rewards.
- Mr. Qi provides special challenges after the relevant progression is unlocked.

# Monsters and Combat

## Overview
Combat occurs mainly in dungeon areas. Monsters can drop useful materials, items, and resources.

## Major combat locations
- The Mines
- Skull Cavern
- Volcano Dungeon
- Mutant Bug Lair
- Other special areas

## Combat equipment
Players can use:
- Weapons
- Boots
- Rings
- Food and buffs
- Other combat-related items

## Monster knowledge for RAG
For each monster, useful fields are:
- monster name
- location
- floor/range
- health
- damage
- defense
- speed
- drops
- special behavior

## RAG examples
Questions the corpus should support:
- "Where can I find this monster?"
- "What does this monster drop?"
- "Which monsters are in the Mines?"
- "Where can I get a specific monster drop?"

# Seasons

## Overview
The game year is divided into four seasons:
- Spring
- Summer
- Fall
- Winter

Each season has its own crops, forage, fish, weather patterns, festivals, and events.

## Spring
Important themes:
- Spring crops
- Spring forage
- Spring fishing
- Egg Festival
- Flower Dance

## Summer
Important themes:
- Summer crops
- Summer forage
- Summer fishing
- Luau
- Dance of the Moonlight Jellies

## Fall
Important themes:
- Fall crops
- Fall forage
- Fall fishing
- Stardew Valley Fair
- Spirit's Eve

## Winter
Important themes:
- Winter forage
- Winter fishing
- Festival of Ice
- Night Market
- Feast of the Winter Star

## Crop season rule
Outdoor crops generally need to match the current season. When a crop's growing season ends, crops that cannot survive the season transition can wither.

## Year structure
A normal season contains 28 days.

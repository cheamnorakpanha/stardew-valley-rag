# Farming

## Overview
Farming is a central gameplay activity. The player prepares soil, plants seeds, waters crops, and harvests mature plants.

## Basic crop workflow
1. Clear an area.
2. Till soil with the Hoe.
3. Plant seeds.
4. Water crops.
5. Continue watering/growing.
6. Harvest when mature.
7. Process or sell the harvest if desired.

## Watering
Crops normally need to be watered each day to continue growing. Rain can provide natural watering.

## Sprinklers
Sprinklers automate watering of nearby crops. Different sprinkler tiers cover different areas.

## Fertilizer
Fertilizer can influence crop quality and/or growth depending on the fertilizer type.

## Scarecrows
Crows can eat outdoor farm crops. Scarecrows protect crops within their effective range.

## Greenhouse
The Greenhouse allows crops to be grown independently of the normal outdoor seasonal cycle.

## Garden Pots
Garden Pots allow indoor crop growing in eligible locations.

## Processing
Common farm processing equipment includes:
- Keg
- Preserves Jar
- Mayonnaise Machine
- Cheese Press
- Loom
- Oil Maker

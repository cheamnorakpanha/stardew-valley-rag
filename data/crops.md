# Crops

## Overview
Crops are plants grown from seeds. On the farm, the normal process is to till soil, plant seeds, water the planted crop, and harvest it when mature. Most crops are seasonal and will wither when their season ends. Some crops regrow after their first harvest.

## Crop categories
Important crop-related concepts include:
- seasonal crops
- regrowing crops
- multi-harvest crops
- giant crops
- special crops
- crops grown in the Greenhouse
- crops grown in Garden Pots

## Common examples
### Parsnip
- Season: Spring
- Growth time: 4 days
- Seed source: Pierre's General Store
- Base sell price: 35g
- Category: Vegetable

### Potato
- Season: Spring
- Growth time: 6 days
- Seed source: Pierre's General Store
- Base sell price: 80g
- Category: Vegetable
- Harvest may yield additional potatoes.

### Pumpkin
- Season: Fall
- Growth time: 13 days
- Seed source: Pierre's General Store and JojaMart
- Base sell price: 320g
- Can become a Giant Crop under the appropriate conditions.

### Blueberry
- Season: Summer
- Growth time: 13 days
- Regrows after harvesting.
- Produces multiple berries at harvest.

### Cranberries
- Season: Fall
- Regrow after harvesting.
- Produce multiple berries.

### Ancient Fruit
- A special crop that can be grown from Ancient Seeds.
- It regrows after harvesting.
- It is useful for high-value artisan production.

### Sweet Gem Berry
- Season: Fall
- Grown from Rare Seeds.
- Seeds are associated with the Traveling Cart.
- The harvested crop is valuable and has special uses.

## Greenhouse
The Greenhouse provides an indoor growing environment. Crops grown there are not constrained by the normal outdoor seasonal cycle.

## Garden Pots
Garden Pots allow crops to be grown indoors in eligible indoor spaces. Crops in Garden Pots are not attacked by crows. Giant Crops cannot be grown in Garden Pots.

## Important crop mechanics
- Crops normally need daily watering to continue growing.
- Sprinklers can automate watering.
- Fertilizer can affect crop quality.
- The Tiller profession increases the sell price of applicable crops.
- Kegs and Preserves Jars can process many crops into artisan goods.

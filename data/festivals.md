# Festivals

## Overview
Festivals are special events that occur on specific days. They can change normal routines and provide unique activities, rewards, shops, or interactions.

## Major festivals
Examples include:
- Egg Festival
- Flower Dance
- Luau
- Dance of the Moonlight Jellies
- Stardew Valley Fair
- Spirit's Eve
- Festival of Ice
- Night Market
- Feast of the Winter Star

## 1.6 festivals
The official 1.6 update added:
- Desert Festival
- Trout Derby
- SquidFest

The Desert Festival is a three-day spring event accessible after the bus is repaired.

## Festival RAG fields
For each festival, store:
- date
- season
- location
- start/end time
- activities
- shops
- rewards
- special requirements
- friendship effects
- unique items
